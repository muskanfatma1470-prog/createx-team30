Core Features

Everything a teacher needs

From submission to verdict in under 2 minutes. No extra tools, no training required.

Adaptive Viva AI

Questions are dynamically generated from the actual submission — not generic templates. Each viva is unique to the student's work.

Difficulty Control

Set Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty. The AI adjusts question depth accordingly — from basic recall to deep conceptual probing.

Teacher Dashboard

Track all students in one view. See class-wide authenticity rates, average scores, and flag suspicious submissions instantly.

PDF Reports

Generate detailed per-student reports with scores, verdict, AI feedback, and recommendations — ready to share or archive.

Session History

All evaluations are saved in-session. Review past results, compare students, and track patterns over time.

3-Level Verdict

Every evaluation ends with a clear verdict: Authentic, Partial Understanding, or Suspicious — with detailed reasoning.
